Field Manual

A tactical game rewards decisions you can understand. This manual turns the mechanics of Coffee Battles into practical lessons you can use in your next match.

Where to begin

Each match begins by choosing a points budget and terrain, then deploying an army made up of regiments. You can build against a computer-controlled army, mirror your composition or work in a custom setup. Once the battle starts, your decisions become orders: move, hold, attack, charge, retreat, fire and change formation. Battles are intentionally short, but a careless attack can still collapse an otherwise strong line.

A three-step first battle

  1. Build a readable force. Start with line infantry, one ranged group and one mobile flanking group. More unit types are not automatically better: they create more different orders to manage.
  2. Deploy with a purpose. Place your line where it can meet an attack without exposing its rear; keep shooting troops away from the first contact and mobile units where they have space to manoeuvre.
  3. Commit deliberately. Select units, issue movement and attack orders, and watch the morale and victory indicators. When the enemy line becomes fixed in a fight, use a second regiment to pressure an open flank rather than piling everything into the front.

The full How to Play guide explains controls, setup steps and victory conditions. The chapters here concentrate on why particular decisions work.
